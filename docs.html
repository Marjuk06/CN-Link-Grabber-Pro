Lossless Keyframe Remuxing

Trims MP4 files using browser-native demuxing and remuxing. Because no video transcoding is performed, trims export in seconds at 100% original quality.

Instant HLS Parallel Download

For online courses using HLS (.m3u8), the Studio fetches the required TS/MP4 segments in parallel and remuxes them instantly without waiting for playback.

Interactive Timeline Scrubber

Drag start and end handles, preview selected loops seamlessly, and adjust millisecond timecodes with precision nudge buttons.

Live Stream Capture Fallback

When direct range fetching is restricted by CORS or blob URLs, the in-page recorder captures video and audio tracks directly from the player.

One-Click PDF Batch Downloader

Select multiple lecture notes or slide handouts and download them all into structured subject folders (e.g., Downloads/CN_Grabber/Physics/).

Pin & Star Priority Vault

Star essential formulas and exam lectures to keep them pinned to the top of your sidebar across pages and browsing sessions.

Multi-Format Academic Exporter

Export resource lists in Raw Text, YouTube Playlist URLs, Notion Markdown Tables, or CSV spreadsheets ready for study planners.
