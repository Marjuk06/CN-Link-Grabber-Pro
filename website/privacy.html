Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026  •  Applies to CN Link Grabber Pro (Chrome, Edge & Firefox)

1. Overview

CN Link Grabber Pro is developed by Marjuk Amin as a client-side academic productivity extension. Protecting your privacy is fundamental to how the extension is built: all page scanning, file grouping, and media trimming occur locally inside your browser.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, log, sell, or transmit your browsing history, visited URLs, or extracted links.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not upload any PDFs, MP4 files, or HLS video segments to remote servers.

3. Local Browser Storage

Your preferences (such as Light/Dark theme selection and items pinned to the Cross-Tab Global Vault) are stored strictly on your device using the browser's storage.local API. You can clear this data anytime from the extension sidebar or by uninstalling the extension.

4. Extension Permissions Explained

  • activeTab & scripting: Inspects the active tab when invoked to locate embedded media, Google Drive links, and PDF resources.
  • downloads: Saves selected lecture PDFs and locally trimmed video clips directly to your device's Downloads folder.
  • clipboardWrite: Copies selected resource lists to your clipboard on user click.
  • storage: Persists local settings and Vault items across browser sessions.
  • webRequest: Passively identifies adaptive media stream manifests (.m3u8, .mpd) and PDF responses on the active tab.

5. Voluntary Support & Feature Requests

If you choose to submit a bug report or feature request through the in-extension Contact modal or the support form on this website, only the name, email address, topic, and message you explicitly enter are sent to the developer (contact@marjukamin.com) for the sole purpose of responding to your request.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email contact@marjukamin.com.
