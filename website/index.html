How It Works

Designed to stay out of your way until you need to grab or organize course files.

1

Open Any Course Page

Navigate to your university portal, LMS, or resource page and click the CN Link Grabber Pro toolbar icon.

2

Review & Filter

The sidebar groups detected videos, streams, PDFs, and Drive links. Use Search or Auto-Scroll for dynamic pages.

3

Save, Export, or Trim

Batch-save PDFs to subject folders, copy formatted notes, or launch the Video Studio to trim MP4/HLS clips locally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about compatibility, privacy, and supported browsers.

Which browsers are supported?

CN Link Grabber Pro supports Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Mozilla Firefox using Manifest V3.

Are my videos or course links uploaded to any server?

No. Link scanning, PDF batching, and MP4/HLS trimming happen strictly inside your browser. No browsing history or extracted URLs leave your device.

How can I request support for my university portal?

Use the Support & Feature Request form below or the Contact button inside the extension sidebar footer, and include the portal domain or layout details.

Is CN Link Grabber Pro free to use?

Yes, it is completely free and open source under the MIT License. Optional tips via Gumroad help cover maintenance and testing.

Privacy Summary

CN Link Grabber Pro is built to respect user privacy. Read the full Privacy Policy.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, track, sell, or share browsing history or extracted webpage links.
  • Local-Only Storage: Theme preferences and Cross-Tab Vault items stay locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local.
  • Local Media Processing: All PDF batch downloads and MP4/HLS trims run directly in your browser without remote server uploads.
  • Voluntary Support Submissions: Contact form submissions are used solely to reply to your support or feature inquiry.
